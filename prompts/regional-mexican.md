## 💡 Pro Tips for Regional Mexican in Suno

### The Non-Negotiables
1. **"Requinto tremolo picking"** — Not just "guitar." The rapid tremolo IS the sound.
2. **"Tuba bassline"** — Not 808, not electric bass. Tuba. Root motion, not melodic.
3. **"Half-time at 140 BPM"** — The tumbados pocket. 70 feel, 140 grid.
4. **"Trap hi-hats 1/32 rolls"** — The urban element. Without it, it's just corridos.
5. **"Raw gritty male vocals"** — Polished = pop. Grit = tumbados.

### Suno Version Guide
| Style | Best Version | Why |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| Classic Tumbados | V5 | Best trap hi-hats, 808 integration |
| Sad Sierreño | V4.5 | Better intimate acoustic, less processing |
| Banda/Brass | V5 | Brass section realism |
| Phonk Fusion | V5 | Cowbell distortion, sidechain precision |
| Reggaeton Fusion | V5 | Dembow rhythm accuracy |
| Instrumental/Loops | V5 | Clean loop points, arrangement control |

### Negative Constraints (Critical)
```
NEGATIVE: no clean electric guitar (must be requinto), no standard drum kit (trap hats only),
no polished pop vocals (must be gritty), no major key unless pop crossover,
no 4-on-the-floor kick (half-time only), no reverb on requinto (dry and upfront),
no synth leads (unless fusion), no quantized-perfect timing (human feel)
```
