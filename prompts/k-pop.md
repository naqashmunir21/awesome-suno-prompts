## 💡 Pro Tips for K-Pop in Suno

### The Non-Negotiables
1. **"Plural vocal layers (5+)"** — K-Pop = group sound. Solo vocal = not K-Pop.
2. **"Point choreography rhythm in chorus"** — The hook must be *danceable*.
3. **"Pre-chorus rapid build with snare roll"** — The tension/release is structural.
4. **"Korean/English mix"** — Pure Korean = domestic. Pure English = Western pop. Mix = K-Pop.
5. **"Polished maximalist production"** — V5 excels here. Don't hold back on layers.

### Version Guide
| Style | Best Version | Why |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| Girl Group Title | V5 | Vocal stacking, polish, chorus impact |
| Boy Group Noise | V5 | Distortion, 808 control, percussion complexity |
| Hyperpop | V5 | Pitch-shift, supersaw, glitch precision |
| Dance Challenge | V5 | Loop precision, hook clarity |
| Ballad/B-side | V4.5 | Intimacy, less processing |
| Acoustic/Unplugged | V4.5 | Natural texture |

### Negative Constraints (Critical)
```
NEGATIVE: no solo vocal (must be group), no raw/lo-fi (unless digicore), 
no standard verse-chorus only (need pre-chorus, post-chorus, dance break),
no "type beat" generic production (must be maximalist polished),
no monotone rap (K-Pop rap = melodic flow), 
no abrupt endings (need outro fade or resolve),
no minor-only for girl groups (need major chorus contrast)
```
