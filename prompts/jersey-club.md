## 💡 Pro Tips for Jersey/Bmore in Suno

### The Non-Negotiables
1. **"Stutter kick pattern: kick-kick-rest-kick"** — Not "fast kicks." The specific pattern: `1& 2& 3 4&`
2. **"Bed squeak vocal chops on 16ths"** — The jersey identifier. Not "vocal chops" generally.
3. **"Aggressive sidechain -12dB"** — The pumping sound is mandatory.
4. **"135 BPM for viral"** — 132 too slow, 138 too fast. 135 = dance challenge pocket.
5. **"30 seconds exactly = 34 bars at 135 BPM"** — Math for perfect loops.

### Version Guide
| Style | Best Version | Why |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| Classic Jersey | V5 | Precise stutter, clean sidechain |
| Bmore Breakbeat | V5 | Breakbeat chopping precision |

### Negative Constraints (Critical)
```
NEGATIVE: no 4-on-the-floor (unless Jersey House), no standard trap hats,
no melodic piano (unless hybrid), no long reverb tails (kills pump),
no slow attack pads (kills energy), no "type beat" generic production,
no verse-chorus standard structure (loops only), 
no clean unmixed sound (sidechain IS the mix)
```
