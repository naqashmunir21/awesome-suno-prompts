```

**Result:** Cheesy, over-sentimental, "AI ballad" clichés

---

```

- **Subtle strings enter at 0:45** → Dynamic evolution
- **Stripped bridge** → Contrast principle

## 📊 Pattern Analysis: What Separates Viral from Generic

| Factor | Generic Prompts | Viral Prompts |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Specific instruments** | 1-2 | 5-8 |
| **Production terms** | 0-1 | 4-7 |
| **Arrangement timestamps** | Never | 60%+ |
| **Genre references** | Broad ("pop") | Micro-genre + era + artist |
| **Vocal detail** | "good vocals" | Style + FX per section |
| **Negative constraints** | Never | 40%+ |
| **Technical specs** | Rare | Always (BPM, Key, Version) |
